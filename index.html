The last-24-hour rainfall value is a weather-model estimate, not a measurement from an on-campus rain gauge. It may differ from rainfall observed locally. The seven-day figure and daily bars are forecast estimates; today's bar may include hours that have already passed. The rain-chance value is the highest hourly probability forecast within the next 24 hours, not the probability that it will rain all day.
